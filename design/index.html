Hand-drawn SVG, not generated images. The jamo's own strokes are the drawing's structure (dark), with just enough context to carry the picture (gold). Crisp at any size, recolourable per theme, and animatable stroke-by-stroke for the learn pages. ~40 of these covers every jamo including compounds.
